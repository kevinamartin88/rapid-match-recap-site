Rapid Match Recap

Rapid Match Recap creates short football match recaps with original narration and graphics. Its automated workflow checks match results, builds videos and thumbnails, and uploads approved recaps to the channel owner's YouTube account.

The YouTube connection is used only by the channel owner. It requests permission to upload videos; visitors to this page do not need to sign in or grant access.
